Charbaman is the hero of Moroccan street drinks, and the giant Charba bottle is rolling after him!

Sprint through five real Moroccan cities in a bright, retro 3D runner inspired by the classic arcade auto-runners of the 90s:

## Questionnaires

### Age rating

- **App Store:** Cartoon or Fantasy Violence: *Infrequent/Mild* (the hero bumps into cars and carts and falls over;
  nothing is hurt or shown hurt). Everything else: *None*. Expected rating: 9+ (answer *None* for violence if you
  judge the bumps too mild to count, which gives 4+).
- **Google Play (IARC):** Violence: cartoon violence, mild, no blood. Users can interact: *yes*, players share only a
  nickname and a score on the leaderboard (no chat, no messages). Shares location: *no*. Digital purchases: *no*.
  Expected: PEGI 3 or 7, ESRB Everyone.

### App Store privacy (App Privacy)

If you turn on the Firebase leaderboard:
- **Data collected:** User Content › *Gameplay Content* (the nickname, score and bottle count a player chooses to post).
- **Linked to the user:** no. **Used for tracking:** no. **Purpose:** App Functionality.

Without Firebase: *Data Not Collected*.

### Google Play Data safety

If you turn on the Firebase leaderboard:
- **Collects:** App activity › *Other user-generated content* (nickname and score, only when the player taps Post score).
- **Shared with third parties:** no (Google Firebase is a service provider). **Optional:** yes.
  **Encrypted in transit:** yes (HTTPS). **Deletion:** on request by email.
- **Purpose:** App functionality.

Without Firebase: *No data collected*.

### Other answers

- **Contains ads:** no. **Target audience:** 6 and up (not designed for children under 13 specifically, so no
  Families programme). **Export compliance / encryption:** none beyond HTTPS
  (`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is already `false` in `app.json`).
